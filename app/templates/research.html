Include in the research brief

01

Reflect, don’t diagnose

Journaling, naming emotions, and making a practical plan can help people notice patterns. Emora never labels a person or provides medical conclusions.

02

Support has boundaries

Companion responses are designed to be warm and useful, while making room for qualified care, real relationships, and emergency support when needed.

03

Insights stay approximate

Saved-message insights use lightweight language signals. They are there for reflection, are not a diagnosis, and can be removed by clearing your history.

04

You own the controls

Export data, delete conversations, remove memories and attachments, or delete your account. Product features should never take those choices away.
