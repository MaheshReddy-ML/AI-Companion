    Can I cancel whenever I want?+

    Yes. Once live billing is connected, you’ll be able to manage or cancel your plan from your account.

    What happens to my private data?+

    Upgrading does not change Emora’s privacy controls. You decide what memories and conversations to keep or remove.

    What happens if I request a downgrade?+

    This preview cannot charge, renew, cancel, or downgrade an account. A future provider-backed downgrade must show its effective date first; saved data remains governed by the same retention and deletion controls, while plan-gated capabilities become locked rather than fabricating or erasing activity.

    When does paid access begin?+

    After a connected payment provider verifies payment, or when an administrator explicitly activates the plan. This preview never grants access from unverified card details.
