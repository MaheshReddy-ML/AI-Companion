BEFORE YOUR FIRST HELLO

A little clarity.

Can I start for free?+

Yes. Begin with text conversations, a private journal, gentle goals, basic insights, and memory controls. Voice and expanded capabilities depend on your plan. Compare plans and checkout availability.

What makes Emora personal?+

Your first meeting collects your preferred name, conversation style, support preferences, and focus areas. These saved choices inform Emora’s conversation context. You can edit them later.

Do I have to share a memory?+

No. Sharing a first memory is optional. You choose what to save and can review or remove it in your workspace.
